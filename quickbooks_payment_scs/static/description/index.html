QuickBooks Payment Gateway

Odoo's QuickBooks Payment Gateway enables customers to conveniently pay with a card. Powered by QuickBooks Secure Gateway, it offers a secure and reliable solution for seamless transactions, making it an ideal choice for businesses seeking an efficient payment option.

Fast and Secure

The module utilizes the QuickBooks Payment Gateway, renowned for its efficiency and security. It leverages advanced encryption to protect data and payment transfers, ensuring transaction safety. Trusted by businesses worldwide, it provides a dependable payment solution.

Accepts Card Payments

It supports various card types for payments on the Odoo website, facilitating seamless transaction processing through the QuickBooks Payment Gateway.

Easy Configurations

Administrators can easily integrate their QuickBooks Payment account with the module.

Odoo QuickBooks Payment Gateway module allows administrators to customize various settings to optimize the payment process.

The module enables administrators to create personalized transactional messages for customers, which will be displayed during the payment process with QuickBooks.

Configure QuickBooks Credentials

Ensure that the status of the QuickBooks payment is set to "Enabled" and "Published" to allow the system to actively use these credentials.

Enter your Merchant ID and Secret Key. These should be provided by the QuickBooks payment gateway.

Choose or create a payment journal that will be used for transactions processed through this QuickBooks integration.

Configure QuickBooks Credentials

  • Ensure that the status of the QuickBooks payment is set to "Enabled" and "Published" to allow the system to actively use these credentials.
  • Enter your Client ID and Client Key and others. These should be provided by the QuickBooks payment gateway.
  • Choose or create a payment journal that will be used for transactions processed through this QuickBooks integration.

Add Product to Cart and Pay Through Safe and Secure Global

  • Navigate to the shop section of the website to choose available products.
  • Select the product you want to purchase from the list of available items.
  • Click the "Add to Cart" button to place the selected product into your shopping cart.
  • Choose your preferred QuickBooks payment method from the available options during payment.
  • Click the "Pay now" button.

Payment Details

  • Enter your credit or debit card number in the designated field.
  • Input the name of the cardholder as it appears on the card.
  • Provide the card's expiration date and the security code (CVV) found on the back of the card.
  • Click the "Pay" button to validate and submit your payment details securely.
  • Your transaction will be processed, and a record of the transaction will be created for tracking and confirmation.

Your Order has been Confirmed

  • After successfully completing your payment through QuickBooks, your order will be processed and confirmed in the system.
  • You will receive a confirmation message or email with details about your order and its status.
