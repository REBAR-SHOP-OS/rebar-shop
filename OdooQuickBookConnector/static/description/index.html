  • Click on the QuickBooks Setting.

  • (In Quickbooks) Go to intuit developer app->keys and credentials and copy your client id and client secret.

  • Paste your client id and client secret in the respective fields.

  • Copy your redirect url.

  • (In Quickbooks) Paste the copied redirect url in the highlighted field in your intuit developer app.

  • Click on the Build Connection.

  • Upon successful authorization you will be shown success page.

  • Select the options you want to sync and click save button.To start sync click "START SYNC" button.

  • Select the Sale Order you want to sync and click on Action Button and then sync to quickbook.

  • Select the Purchase Order you want to sync and click on Action Button and then sync to quickbook.

  • Select the Invoices you want to sync and click on Action Button and then sync to quickbook.

  • Select the Bill you want to sync and click on Action Button and then sync to quickbook.

  • Select the credit note you want to sync and click on Action Button and then sync to quickbook.

  • Select the payment you want to sync and click on Action Button and then sync to quickbook.

  • To reconcile the Journal Entry with payment post the Journal Entry by selecting the Journal Entry and then click "Action" button and select "Post Entries" option.

  • Click on "Post Journal Entries" button.

  • The journal Entry is now posted.

  • Now post the Payments by selecting the Payment and click on Action button and then select post payment option.

  • Payment is now posted.

  • Reconcile the Journal Entry with Payment by selecting the Journal Entry and then click "Action" Button and select "Reconcile" option.

  • Selected Journal Item is now reconciled and its payment status is visible.

  • To complete the Payment reconciling process select the payment and click "Action" Button and select "Reconcile" option.

  • Journal Entry is fully Reconciled.
